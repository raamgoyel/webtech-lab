About the System

Objectives

The main aim is to read urine test strips quickly and help doctors find early signs of disease.


Working Principle

The strip is dipped in urine and the colours are checked. The system then matches the colours with known values to give the result.


Benefits

It saves time, reduces mistakes, and helps the hospital give faster care to patients.


A doctor said: This system makes urine testing easier and more reliable for our patients.
