Urine Test Strip Reader

The Urine Test Strip Reader checks urine samples for signs of infection and other conditions.

Important test parameters include glucose, protein, and leukocytes.

Other parameters are UTI, RBC, WBC, pH, and SG.

Glucose is measured in mg/dL. Example value: 102 mg/dL. Water formula is H2O.

Wash your hands before handling the test strip.

Do not touch the reagent pads on the strip.

This information is for educational use only and is not a medical diagnosis.
